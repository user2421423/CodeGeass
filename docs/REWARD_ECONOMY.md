# Persistent Victory Rewards (October 2026)

All rewards are **one-time per reward key**, with clean hundreds of command tokens and multiples of ten Elite fragments. Elite fragments are no longer distributed to every unit of a faction.

## Rarity restrictions

Rarity is determined by Elite Force combat class:

| Rarity | Combat classes | Total | Per faction |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rare | Raider, Medium Armor, Amphibious | 12 | 3 |
| Epic | Heavy Armor, Rocket Artillery, Siege Artillery | 12 | 3 |
| Legendary | Super Heavy Armor | 4 | 1 |

The permanent HQ Elite roster consists of 28 units across Britannia, Black Knights, Europia United, and the Chinese Federation. Euro Britannia still uses Britannian Elite Forces for battlefield deployment, but its **Story Mode three-star fragment rewards offer Europia United Elite Forces**. Rarity standardization does **not** alter class stats, combat abilities, upgrade prices, accumulated fragments, HQ levels, or previously claimed rewards. Unclaimed Story and Conquest reward pools are re-evaluated using current mission-based reward eligibility and rarity, including earlier saved awards; claimed rewards remain unchanged. Unclaimed World Campaign rewards continue to refresh globally.

- **Normal:** choose one **Rare** Elite Force.
- **Hard:** choose one **Epic** Elite Force.
- **Challenge:** choose one **Legendary** Elite Force.
- **Elite:** choose any one Rare, Epic, or Legendary Elite Force.

Story Mode and Conquest fragment choices are faction-eligible only. **World Campaign fragment choices may target any faction's non-scenario-only Elite Force** matching the difficulty's rarity restriction. Pending selections are saved to the permanent HQ profile and available from results screens, World Campaign and HQ → Elite Forces. A selection can be claimed once, never repeatedly. The selected Elite receives the entire bundle.

## Story Mode

| Difficulty | Total tokens at 1★ | Total at 2★ | Total at 3★ | Elite fragments |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 100 | 100 | 200 | 10 Rare at 3★ |
| Hard | 200 | 200 | 300 | 10 Epic at 3★ |
| Challenge | 300 | 300 | 400 | 10 Legendary at 3★ |
| Elite | 400 | 400 | 500 | 10 of any rarity at 3★ |

Stars and tokens are cumulative **per mission and difficulty**, paying only positive differences when improving a score. A 2★ victory never awards fragments. A first-ever 3★ performance for each difficulty earns a single fragment bundle.

Story Mode campaign milestones: **100 tokens** at 50% stars; **200 tokens** at 75%; **300 tokens** at 100% (600 maximum for the campaign). Milestones award no fragments and are paid once. Story missions **never grant a free permanent HQ Elite Force level**. Cornelia’s Gloucester and Guren Mk-II can each be unlocked with fragments like any other Elite Force, without completing a specific Story Mode mission. Players who already own these Elites keep their existing levels and fragments.

## World Campaign

Each objective/difficulty pays tokens and one selected bundle once. **The winner may choose any faction's elite of the appropriate rarity**, regardless of the faction played in the World Campaign. Saved unclaimed World Campaign bundles also offer the expanded choices; already claimed rewards are unchanged. Map territory itself remains single-completion progression.

| Difficulty | First victory tokens | Fragments |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 200 | 10 Rare |
| Hard | 300 | 10 Epic |
| Challenge | 400 | 10 Legendary |
| Elite | 500 | 20 of any rarity |

Completing **each tier** pays **500** bonus tokens. Completing **all tiers of one faction's World Campaign** pays an additional **1,000** tokens. Tier and overall completion rewards are not repeatable.

## Conquest

| Difficulty | First victory tokens | Fragments |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 500 | 20 Rare |
| Hard | 1,000 | 20 Epic |
| Challenge | 1,500 | 20 Legendary |
| Elite | 2,000 | 30 of any rarity |

The first **ever** Conquest victory adds **200** tokens. Each era/faction/difficulty combination pays once. Replays grant no tokens or fragments. The banked-science conversion reward has been removed.

## Elite Force upgrade requirements

| Upgrade | Cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Unlock Level 1 | 30 fragments |
| Level 1 → 2 | 40 |
| Level 2 → 3 | 50 |
| Level 3 → 4 | 60 |
| Level 4 → 5 | 70 |
| **Total locked → Lv.5** | **250** |

Existing owned Elites and saved fragments are preserved; balances are not reset or rewritten. The four amphibious Rare elites (Portman II, Panzer-Frosch II, Shui Gun-Ru II, and Black Knights Gekka Amphibious II) share these costs. Their combat abilities unlock at Lv.3 and improve at Lv.5; the Lv.5 percentages replace their Lv.3 values rather than stacking.
