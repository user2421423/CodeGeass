# Persistent Victory Rewards (October 2026)

All rewards are **one-time per reward key**, with clean hundreds of command tokens and multiples of ten Elite fragments. Elite fragments are no longer distributed to every unit of a faction.

## Rarity restrictions

- **Normal:** choose one **Rare** Elite Force.
- **Hard:** choose one **Epic** Elite Force.
- **Challenge:** choose one **Legendary** Elite Force.
- **Elite:** choose any one Rare, Epic, or Legendary Elite Force.

Only faction-eligible, non-scenario-only Elite Forces appear in the selector. Pending selections are saved to the permanent HQ profile and available from results screens, World Campaign and HQ → Elite Forces. A selection can be claimed once, never repeatedly. The selected Elite receives the entire bundle.

## Story Mode

| Difficulty | Total tokens at 1★ | Total at 2★ | Total at 3★ | Elite fragments |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 100 | 100 | 200 | 10 Rare at 3★ |
| Hard | 200 | 200 | 300 | 10 Epic at 3★ |
| Challenge | 300 | 300 | 400 | 10 Legendary at 3★ |
| Elite | 400 | 400 | 500 | 10 of any rarity at 3★ |

Stars and tokens are cumulative **per mission and difficulty**, paying only positive differences when improving a score. A 2★ victory never awards fragments. A first-ever 3★ performance for each difficulty earns a single fragment bundle.

Story Mode campaign milestones: **100 tokens** at 50% stars; **200 tokens** at 75%; **300 tokens** at 100% (600 maximum for the campaign). Milestones award no fragments and are paid once. Existing guaranteed third-mission Level 1 unlocks for **Cornelia’s Gloucester** and **Guren Mk II** are unchanged.

## World Campaign

Each objective/difficulty pays tokens and one selected bundle once. Map territory itself remains single-completion progression.

| Difficulty | First victory tokens | Fragments |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 200 | 10 Rare |
| Hard | 300 | 10 Epic |
| Challenge | 400 | 10 Legendary |
| Elite | 500 | 20 of any rarity |

Completing **each tier** pays **500** bonus tokens. Completing **all tiers of one faction's World Campaign** pays an additional **1,000** tokens. Tier and overall completion rewards are not repeatable.

## Conquest

| Difficulty | First victory tokens | Fragments |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal | 500 | 20 Rare |
| Hard | 1,000 | 20 Epic |
| Challenge | 1,500 | 20 Legendary |
| Elite | 2,000 | 30 of any rarity |

The first **ever** Conquest victory adds **200** tokens. Each era/faction/difficulty combination pays once. Replays grant no tokens or fragments. The banked-science conversion reward has been removed.
