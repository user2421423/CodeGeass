# Commander progression and Commission design

**Military rank:** Active combat and Mobility stars begin capped at 2 for Second/First Lieutenant. The cap increases to 3 at Captain, 4 at Lieutenant Colonel, 5 at Brigadier General, and 6 at Lieutenant General; it stays unchanged at intervening ranks. Tactical Command runs from Second Lieutenant to Major, Operational Command from Lieutenant Colonel to Major General, and High Command from Lieutenant General to Supreme Commander. Rank does not determine generic skill slots.

**Commission:** Standard Commission has 1 generic skill slot, Distinguished 2, Legendary 3. All commissions may rank up; signature abilities do not consume generic slots. Initial Commission replaces storyTier and commander rarity stars, while campaign-mission stars and Elite Force levels remain separate.

**Global High Command:** The persistent player profile starts with 2 High Command positions, shared across factions. Every owned commander of rank Lieutenant General, General or Supreme Commander occupies one. There is no one-Supreme-Commander rule. Purchase extra positions up to a capacity of 10: the third costs 1,000 tokens, fourth 2,000, fifth 3,000, sixth 4,000, seventh 5,000, eighth 6,000, ninth 7,000 and tenth 8,000 (36,000 total from 2 to 10). Each upgrade is permanent and shared across factions; previously purchased capacities are retained. The Conquest Challenge Pass final 1,600-CP reward grants one additional permanent High Command position, allowing up to 11 total (10 purchased positions plus the pass bonus). The bonus does not advance or discount the purchase ladder. Previously completed 1,600-CP milestones also count.

**Retirement:** Retire a High Command officer to Major General, refund 90% of recorded costs for the rank promotions and purchased sixth stars forfeited above Major General's five-star limit, and release a position. There is no extra retirement fee. Commission level and generic skills remain intact. Permanent decorations and service citations are unaffected by retirement. Legacy spending without an itemized ledger can only be estimated from the old rank/initial ratings.

**Commission upgrades:** Permanent Standard → Distinguished → Legendary upgrades have engine validation and UI scaffolding. Standard → Distinguished costs 200 tokens; Distinguished → Legendary costs 350 tokens (550 total). These purchases are permanent and are not refunded when an officer retires from High Command. Current recruitment, rank, star, generic skill and research costs remain unchanged.

**Personal commander assignment cost:** Battle credits are charged once per assignment, using `100 + 40 × commission index + 30 × military rank stage` (Standard/Distinguished/Legendary commission indexes 0/1/2; Tactical/Operational/High Command stages 0/1/2). This yields Standard 100/130/160, Distinguished 140/170/200, and Legendary 180/210/240 credits by rank stage. Unit type, Elite status, and individual character do not change the price. Promotions and Commission upgrades change the cost of future assignments, not existing deployments. Scripted operation commanders begin assigned without an assignment fee. This cost uses battle credits, not permanent HQ command tokens. The assignment interface displays only the total price.

**Movement:** Conquest and Scenarios give non-warship units +2 movement; Story Mode has no universal +2 bonus. Mobility gives +1 movement per active star, following the same 2/3/4/5/6 rank thresholds at Second Lieutenant/Captain/Lieutenant Colonel/Brigadier General/Lieutenant General. The Mobility cap also applies to fixed operation commanders; their combat ratings remain fixed and uncapped by rank. Recruitment screens display movement computed from current operation rules and research, with Elite Force bonuses where applicable.

**Migration:** Existing personal ratings above their rank's limits remain recorded but inactive until promoted. The once-only profile migration resolves existing global High Command over-cap situations by keeping the two highest-ranked officers, then demoting the remainder with a full refund of estimable forfeited purchases. New persistent commander records start at Second Lieutenant with Tactical-limited ratings; fixed operation commanders remain unchanged.

**Military honors:** Medals and ribbons are purely commemorative, belong to the recruited HQ commander who earned them, and have no equipment slots or stat modifiers. Tactical battlefield achievements, campaign service and the three conquest Laurels are saved as permanent citations. Scripted operation officers receive operation-only recognition. Historical shared-inventory and equipped medals are preserved in Legacy Honors with no invented recipient. See `docs/MILITARY_HONORS.md`.

**Implementation:** Source files in src/engine are deployed via the standard build_sources scripts to create ignored dist/engine.js. Commander data and UI files in dist are tracked. Do not replace this build step with a hand-edited dist/engine.js.
