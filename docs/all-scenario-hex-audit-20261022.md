# All-scenario terrain audit — 22 October 2026

## Scope

Every one of the **68 registered scenarios** was instantiated for **every playable side** and checked for stable terrain; **1,200,612 scenario hexes (counting each separate scenario map)** were inspected. In total, **1,103,460 hexes** had a centre-point land-versus-sea comparison to an independent Natural Earth 1:50m land polygon dataset.

The audit checks supported terrain identifiers, tile/grid coordinates, faction-side terrain agreement, city and port placement, ship navigability, and illegal blocked-hex deployment. Sea, mixed coast, playable land and deliberately nonplayable `offmap` are separately counted. **Final structural errors: 0.**

## Corrections committed to main

- **Partition of Britannia:** replace unsupported internal `tundra` identifier with the supported `snow` identifier (which the game displays as tundra) for **2,027 hexes**, including intended legacy-save terrain references. Commit `f3d16d2b5f161a9877899a8456350b4f448b29ae`.
- **Alternate history:** preserve faction-correct flying airships during force conversions; never convert airships to ordinary sea-only carriers and strand them on land. This resolves the Chinese Civil War and Knights of the Round Rebellion deployment failures. Commit `d46cae77bda3002ac8075883ed67bf1bf53494c7`.
- **Battle for Xinjiang:** stop city/army nearest-hex selection from using impassable lake/peak cells. Commit `ac43b5d913b98cf629774b76acac27be92c2f735`.

## Follow-up confirmed cartographic fixes (committed to `main`)

The post-audit follow-up identified foreign countries erroneously treated as navigable sea or capturable territory in three regional scenarios. Reproducible Natural Earth 1:50m **admin-0 country masks** now exclude only foreign-country ground while preserving playable nation/region, city anchors, ports and explicitly modelled waterways:

- **North African Offensive:** reclassified **1,570** foreign-country cells as blocked `offmap` rather than sea (Spain, Italy, Greece, Turkey and other foreign shorelines). All **23 ports** remain valid.
- **Crossing the Suez Canal:** excluded **2,015** foreign-country cells (Israel, Palestine, Jordan and Saudi Arabia) from navigable/playable terrain while preserving the intentionally widened canal and **14 ports**.
- **Invasion of France:** excluded **576** foreign-country cells previously classified as Channel/Atlantic water or playable coast (notably British coastline); all **11 ports** remain valid.

These changes are generated by `tools/build_north_africa_foreign_mask.py`, `tools/build_suez_foreign_mask.py` and `tools/build_france_foreign_mask.py`. The [Mediterranean landmask workflow](https://github.com/user2421423/CodeGeass/actions/runs/39734104426) rebuilt and successfully reran the scenario-wide structural audit. The reference comparison is now **22,225** raw disagreements, down from **24,953**, with **0 structural failures**. Remaining differences include deliberately artificial waterways, freshwater lakes absent from the land-only reference, coastline-resolution differences and purposely excluded regions; they have **not** been auto-mutated.

Audit script: `tools/audit_all_scenario_hexes.cjs`. [Successful complete audit run](https://github.com/user2421423/CodeGeass/actions/runs/39729895635).

## Extended review of the other scenarios

A second source-level geographic investigation examined the previously uncorrected regional map generators and the per-scenario mismatch candidates. Five additional **confirmed** physical classification problems were corrected on `main`:

- **Battle of Beijing:** hand-drawn Bohai and Liaodong boundary replaced by a nine-sample Natural Earth land mask. Reference disagreements went from 1,475 to **2**; all **11 ports** valid.
- **Invasion of Korea:** physical land in the adjoining Chinese/Russian mainland had been partly navigable sea and partly erroneously playable. **3,052** hexes are now excluded `offmap`; reference disagreements went from 978 to **54**; all **28 ports** valid.
- **Invasion of the Caucasus:** Sea of Azov and Black Sea polygon corrected using independent land boundaries. The **Caspian remains in the separate original regional basin**; disagreements dropped from 1,154 to **650** (much of the residual arises because land-only reference polygons do not record the Caspian as water); **12 ports** valid.
- **Invasion of Manchuria:** corrected the mistaken assumption that everything above 43.5° N is land in the northeastern Sea of Japan viewport. **54 open-sea** and **13 mixed-coast** hexes accurately classified; disagreements 336 to **276**; **3 ports** valid.
- **Invasion of India:** restored **105** northeast mainland hexes around the eastern Assam/map boundary erroneously classified as sea by a simplified regional polygon. Disagreements 498 to **393**; **26 ports** valid.

These maps can be regenerated from `tools/build_beijing_landmask.py`, `tools/build_korea_foreign_mask.py`, `tools/build_caucasus_watermask.py`, `tools/build_manchuria_north_coast.py`, and `tools/build_india_northeast_land.py` using the independent Natural Earth geographic inputs. [Successful second complete audit (68 scenarios, all player sides)](https://github.com/user2421423/CodeGeass/actions/runs/39737624644).

The remaining scenarios were also instantiated, checked against the independent land reference at the hex level, and compared to their source map rules. **All 68 scenarios pass the structural terrain and deployment checks.** The per-scenario table below is refreshed from the latest full audit run.

**Unresolved reference disagreements are not automatically terrain errors.** Major categories include the Great Lakes and Caspian inland water that the land-only reference does not detect, deliberately excluded geographic land in the Area 18 map, deliberately widened canals/rivers, world-map Arctic coastline simplifications and different GSHHS versus Natural Earth shore resolution. Independent city ports placed some hexes away from their parent city are intentional in several scenarios, not automatically invalid. A definitive manually certified every-coastline polygon match is not claimed; these residual candidates should not be batch-converted without map-specific gameplay review.

## Important accuracy distinction

The independent Natural Earth point comparison reports **19,159 raw geographic disagreements**, which **must not be presented as confirmed bugs or silently mass-converted**. That geodata excludes inland lakes (e.g. the Great Lakes and Caspian), while scenarios also intentionally crop playable countries, use fictional coast/canal crossings, explicit GSHHS 20–80% mixed-coast footprints, or reserve background `offmap` cells. Coastline edge pixels differ further between data sources. The independent comparison identifies candidates for map-specific cartographic review; it is not a definitive validation of every external coast. The structural and deployment audit **passed**, but an independently certified cartographic match for every hex is **not claimed**. No unverified broad terrain remapping was made.

The `Reference mismatches` column below counts independent centre-point land/sea disagreements (not verified defects); coastline cells and nonplayable background cells are excluded from that direct comparison.
