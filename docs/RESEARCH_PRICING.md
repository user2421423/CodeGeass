# HQ Research Price and Victory Progression

## Standardized purchase costs

Every technology in all six branches uses exactly the same incremental price by upgrade level:

| Level being purchased | Command tokens |
|---|---:|
| I | 100 |
| II | 200 |
| III | 300 |
| IV | 400 |
| V | 500 |

The game has **76 technologies with 200 purchasable levels**, costing **38,600 command tokens** in total. Costs depend on purchase level, not on branch, technological strength, or research-unlock tier. A two-level technology costs 300 tokens to complete; three levels cost 600; four cost 1,000; and five cost 1,500. Existing research bonuses, prerequisites, maximum levels, and research tiers are unchanged.

**Legacy purchases:** HQ research version remains 2, so no saved technology levels are reset. Previously paid amounts are not refunded or recalculated; only future purchases use the new price table.

## Qualifying victories for research tiers

Research requirements remain unchanged: **0** qualifying victories for Tier I, **2** for Tier II, **4** for Tier III, and **7** for Tier IV.

Count exactly one credit per:

1. A distinct rewarded Conquest victory, using the existing Conquest victory counter and unique map/faction/difficulty records.
2. An **entire Story Mode campaign** completed at **at least 1★ in every mission**, independent of difficulty. Each story arc counts once.
3. An **entire World Campaign** with **every tier and every objective completed** for that faction. Each faction's overarching World Campaign counts once.

Individual Story Mode missions, individual World Campaign regional battles, World Campaign tiers, and replaying campaigns **do not** provide extra research-tier credits. They can still pay their approved token and fragment rewards.

The game imports previously completed Story Mode and World Campaign progression automatically from their existing persistent records when loading the HQ profile. Earned completion credits are permanent and are never double-counted by replay or by reloading the same progress. Partial campaigns do not count.

HQ → Research displays the combined total, with separate Conquest, Story Mode, and World Campaign counts.
