# Scenario starting resources — audited 22 October 2026

All **68 regional scenarios**, including alternate histories. Figures come from the game's scenario constructors and `Knightmare.income` with **Normal difficulty**, fixed seed `246801`, and the **first listed playable faction** selected. The recently updated 28-city *Defense of Japan* scenario is included.

**Income columns** are credits, industry, science and Sakuradite generated **per turn** at the beginning of the scenario. **Stockpile columns** show starting reserves in those same resources. These are different: a faction with no cities can have zero recurring income but a large opening war chest.

Income is measured after application of the fixed scenario city rosters. Original authored starting reserves are preserved; this roster adjustment does **not** globally rescale stocks. Selecting another player faction or a harder difficulty can change situational bonuses and opening reserves, especially for enemy factions. Conquest is out of scope for the 1,000-credit scenario cap.

## Audit summary

- 68 scenarios and 145 starting faction positions reviewed.
- 32 scenarios use fixed authored city-exclusion rosters; a total of 608 starting cities are excluded.
- Highest starting **credit income**: 1,000 per turn. No audited Normal-difficulty position exceeds the 1,000-credit target.
- 43 starting faction positions generate **more than 1,000 industry** per turn; industry was not part of the agreed credit-only cap.
- 14 faction positions have zero recurring credits, normally expeditionary cityless invasions.

## Recommended follow-ups

1. Balance **industry** separately if desired: the 1,000-credit constraint should not silently become a limit on unrelated resources. Very large industry bases remain after city pruning, especially in India, Canada and Mexico.
2. Review the size of **opening reserves** for late-game operations. Some 0-income invading factions hold large pre-funded war chests by design; those should be balanced against the cost of transport and their first objective.
3. Continue protecting **city coverage, ports, capitals and commander deployment hubs**. Fixed exclusions now replace the earlier city-scoring algorithm, with province and army-group references remapped for excluded cities.
4. For balance comparisons across player selections, run a separate faction/difficulty matrix. This document is a Normal-difficulty opening-state snapshot for each model's first playable faction.
